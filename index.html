Radial D-pad keyboard · interactive test

Idea: text entry with only the four arrow keys of a TV remote. Every character is a 4×4×4 tree: the first press picks one of 4 sectors, the second one of 4 groups, the third one of 4 characters. Exactly 3 presses per character, no OK needed to select, no timeouts, learnable blind.

Use your keyboard to emulate a remote: ↑ ↓ ← → choose a sector. Enter (OK): short press in the root = space, long press (0.7 s) = confirm. Backspace (Back): erase a character / go up one level; hold to auto-repeat; on an empty field it closes the keyboard. M (Menu): short = next layout, long = layout picker. Esc (Home): close and discard.

On a phone / with a mouse: tap the four sectors for the arrows, and use the OK / Back / Menu / Home buttons below. Press and hold works the same as on a keyboard (the button fills while you hold).

Feedback welcome: how does it feel after a minute of typing? Is the long-press logic sensible on a real remote? Would you group letters differently (e.g. by frequency instead of alphabetically)?
